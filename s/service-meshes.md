# Service meshes

A `service mesh` is dedicated infrastructure that manages communication among your organisation’s [microservices](../m/microservice-architectures.md).

A service mesh provides common services like:
- authentication and authorisation
- service discovery
- retries
- circuit breaking
- routing
- load balancing
- encryption (mTLS)
- rate limiting
- monitoring and logging.
