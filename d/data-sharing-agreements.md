# Data sharing agreements

A `data sharing agreement` is a formal document that sets out the legal, operational, and security arrangements for sharing data between two or more organisations. 
It is a 'rulebook' that helps ensure that data is shared lawfully, securely, and only for agreed purposes.

A data sharing agreement should define:
- purpose - Why is the data being shared? What can it be used for?
- parties - Which organisations are providing and receiving the data?
- data - What specific datasets, fields, or types of information are being shared?
- legal basis - What legislation, statutory powers, or GDPR lawful basis permits the sharing?
- roles and responsibilities - Which parties are data controllers, joint controllers, or data processors?
- security - What are the required encryption requirements, access controls, storage requirements, and incident management procedures?
- retention and disposal - How long can the data be kept? How must it be securely deleted?
- onward sharing - Can the recipient share the data with others?
- breach reporting and data subject rights - How will privacy incidents and GDPR requests be handled?

A data sharing agreement provides **evidence** that data sharing has been properly considered and governed, demonstrating compliance with UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018.
